## Do not say

Cosmetic claims only — these phrases appear in the brand's own copy but should not be repeated in paid creative without substantiation:

- "clinically proven"
- "treat"

Standing constraints: no before/after implying permanent change, no disease or condition-treatment language, no unearned "dermatologist recommended".
